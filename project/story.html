The Story So Far

The Bloody Butchers are a ragtag pirate crew charged by "The King of the Seas" Davey Jones to prevent the end of the world at the hands of the four Mythical Horse-Horse Fruits of the Apocalypse.

Prologue: Deep Sea Horror

The crew met when they all, for some reason or another, were contacted by "Deep Sea Horror" Beckham Ford, one of the Emperors of the Sea at the time, to work aboard one of his ships. On his ship the crew witnessed him utilize his devilfruit, the Fish-Fish Fruit Model: Davey Jones in a battle against Marine Vice Admiral "Selfish Justice" Shironezu. In the battle, Beckam Ford seemed to die and the ship sank.

After being thrown overboard, the crew all miraculously washed ashore on an island, along with Marine Engineer Lotus, whom they pressured into joining the crew. The crew also mysteriously had vivre cards for Beckham Ford and Davey Jones, suggesting that they were both alive and different entities.

Arc One: Lilipadd Island

After finding our way to a town called Indigo Village on the island, the crew then encountered their cook Cas' ex-husband and Emperor of the Sea, "Unbreakable" Victual Bastion, who offered to give us a ride on his ship, much to Cas' displeasure.

On his ship, the crew was told that the Glasswerk pirates were seeking out the Quetzalcoatl sword that belonged to Beckham Ford, could be used to control the weather, and was hidden on Lilypad Island.

In the brig of Bastion's ship, they met Lamb, a bounty hunter and angsty teenager that had been thrown in there after attempting to kill Bastion. He claimed that he was looking for something on Lilypad Island, and that he could lead the crew to Beckham Ford's sword as long as they broke him out of the brig and let him get what he was looking for. The crew agreed to this.

On the journey to Lilypad Island, the crew decided that they would call themselves the Bloody Butchers, despite Cas’ wishes, in reference to the infamous nickname he had as a young and violent pirate.

Once the newly named Bloody Butchers arrived at Lilypad Island, the crew broke Lamb out of the brig and separated from the Glasswerk pirates.

After navigating the jungle and entering Davey Jones' Locker (losing track of Lamb in the process), the crew found Steven, a living human skull and arms that they added to their crew.

At the end of the Locker, they found the Quetzalcoatl sword as well as a dagger that could separate a devilfruit from a person or object it was attached to.

After exiting Davey Jones' Locker, the crew was surrounded by Marines led by, now Admiral, Shironezu. After the crew, especially their diplomat Calliope, thoroughly disrespected her, the Glasswerk pirates arrived and attacked the Marines, allowing the Bloody Butchers to escape.

In the jungle, they found "Fourth Horseman" Lamb. The object he was looking for was the Horse-Horse Fruit Model: Death, which he had eaten. He thanked the crew for their assistance before he threatened them and left.

The crew then stole a Marine Vice-Admiral's ship from the harbor and sailed away from Lilypad Island.

First Interlude: The Man Whose Dream it was to Destroy the World

The Bloody Butchers sailed to the North Blue and got their ship, now named the Cadaver's Cleaver, renovated such that they could disguise their ship as a Marine ship when they wanted, but otherwise could have a pirate ship suited to their tastes.

They decided to follow the Davey Jones vivre card and returned to an area nearby Lilypad Island where Davey Jones and the Flying Dutchman appeared to them.

Davey Jones explained that while he was alive, he killed a man whose dream it was to destroy the world. That man became the Horse-Horse Fruit Model: Death. He also explained that the Bloody Butchers were responsible for releasing the Death Fruit onto the world again and that Lamb had likely been consumed by the Fruit.

The Death Fruit was the only thing that could kill Davey Jones, so he wanted it destroyed. He told the crew to catch Lamb and bring him to Jones so he could sever the Fruit from Lamb's body and destroy it before the Fruit could destroy the world.

Arc Two: Sailor's Haven

To do this, the crew decided to create a vivre card for Lamb using a hair that we had in our possession and set sail for Sailor's Haven, a pirate town where they could get it made by an old friend of Cas'.

In Sailor's Haven the crew received news of the location of "Crustacean King" Gibbs Kanijiru, the father of the Bloody Butcher’s helmsman Gibbs; that the adoptive father of the crew’s Navigator, Pleco, "Legendary" Tabernacle N. Getralt's Warlord status had been revoked; that the Glasswerk Pirates won in their battle against the Marines, and that a new Emperor of the Sea, "The Collector" Allison Ceasar, had been declared following the disappearance of Beckham Ford.

Carter Tography, the son of Cas' old friend Tographer, agreed to make the vivre card for Lamb for the crew if they could collect some kind of DNA material from Allison Ceasar.

Calliope planned to seduce Allison Ceasar, but in the end she acquired a lock of hair from him willingly by impressing him with her guts in a high-stakes game of poker.

Successful, the Bloody Butchers returned to Carter Tography and had our Lamb vivre card and an Allison Ceasar card made before leaving Sailor’s Haven.

Second Interlude: Power to Tear te World Apart at its Seams

Through long distance conversation with Davey Jones and a devilfruit encyclopedia, the crew learned more about the four Mythical Horse-Horse Fruits: Conquest, War, Famine, and Death. Each Fruit was independently powerful, but the unification of all four would have enough power to tear the world apart at its seams.

Davey Jones also advised the crew to rely on other strong pirates who can be negotiated with.

Feeling unprepared to try catching Lamb and unsure of what to do otherwise, the crew decided to follow Allison Ceasar’s vivre card and speak with him again.

On their way to his hideout on the Grand Line, the Bloody Butchers were attacked by an unknown Warlord of the Sea with a segmented body, but managed to escape.

Then, they arrived at Allison Ceasar’s hideout and Calliope spoke with him. He told her about Warlord of the Sea “All Knowing” Galileo, a robot and information broker that might know how to control or contain the power of the Horse-Horse Fruit Model: Death.

Galileo returns to Egghead Island regularly, so the crew decided to sail there to find him.

Arc Three: Egghead Island

Upon arriving to Egghead Island, the crew got into a fight and killed several Marines.

Galileo’s pirate airship arrived at Egghead Island and the crew got up to it by popping a World Government Coast Guard dingy and being launched upward.

They asked Galileo for information on the Horse-Horse Fruit Model: Death, but he demanded information from them in return (Cas’ classic pirate knowledge apparently wasn’t good enough).

Calliope then seduced Galileo, thus teaching him about love and satisfying his demand for knowledge.

From Galileo and documents on his airship the crew learned: seastone stakes kept in the deepest parts of the Marine’s archives were the most likely thing to be able to contain the Horse-Horse Fruit Model: Death’s power, Getralt was arrested for conspiring against the World Government and was probably being held in the mobile Marine prison in the East Blue, and information about the powers and likely current whereabouts of the four Mythical Horse-Horse Fruits.

However, Galileo also used the power of the Data-Data Fruit to extract information from the Bloody Butchers. So, he then knew everything that the crew did, including things like the existence of Davey Jones, each of their tragic backstories, and many excellent recipes.

Third Interlude: Go Get Them

After leaving Galileo's airship, the crew worried a bit over the possibility of Galileo knowing far more about them and about what they knew than they would like him to.

The crew also waffled over what to do next, either searching out the seastone stakes or seeking Pleco's father Getralt to break him out of marine prison.

They wrote to Davey Jones to warn him about the possibility of the World Government knowing about his existence, the activity of the Horse-Horse Fruit Model: Death, and their mission through Galileo and asked if they should get the seastone stakes.

The crew received a very prompt and urgent response to "GO GET THEM" and set course for the Marine Archives at Marine Base Borealis.

Arc Four: Marine Base Borealis

The Bloody Butchers arrived to Marine Base Borealis impersonating a Vice Admiral’s ship to get past security using information from Lotus and some convenient ponegliph rubbings that they had.

Once inside, the crew split up, Pleco going down to the Marine Archives to locate the seastone stakes while the rest of the group maintained their cover and went with an unfamiliar marine admiral to his office on the prison level of the base.

On the prison level, they found Getralt weakened and imprisoned and freed him while the marine admiral's back was turned. Getralt immediately healed from his injuries and instigated battle with the marine admiral.

The crew, reuniting with Pleco, fled the battle.

Getralt briefly reunited with his son Pleco and received the "sword" Nosferatu that Pleco had been tasked with keeping safe, combining it with a long handle to reveal its true form as a polearm before he returned to battle against the massive Ursa Major form of the marine admiral.

The crew escaped on the Cadaver's Cleaver and watched as Marine Base Borealis crumbled into the sea, leaving the fate of Getralt and all of the treasures from the Marine Archives unknown.

They were then attacked by the gigantic warship of Marine Admiral "Adamant Justice" Kuroryu. However, the Glasswerk pirates, led by Bastion, appeared to assist them. The Bloody Butchers goaded Kuroryu into leaping towards their ship in order to give the Glasswerk pirates an opening for their crew to fight the marines and Bastion to fight Kuroryu.

The Bloody Butchers used the opportunity to flee the battle, and they watched as a massive explosion caused by "The Shriek" Echo destroyed the warship, leaving the status of the Glasswerk pirates unknown.

Fourth Interlude: Claw to Claw

After the dust settled and the coast was clear, the Bloody Butchers returned to the wreckage of Marine Base Borealis to search for evidence of Getralt's fate and valuable items from the archives.

The remnants of Getralt's conquerors haki indicated that he did somehow leave the wreckage alive. From the depths, Gibbs and Calliope recovered the Dog-Dog fruit model: Hyena and a silver-bladed sword that mysteriously produces blood that they later discovered was the Horse-Horse Fruit Model: War.

Now that the crew had the War fruit, they knew that the Horseman of Death was certain to be after them to collect it and further his plans to destroy the world. The crew planned to take the War fruit to Davey Jones in the hopes that his power would be enough to keep the fruit away from Death.

A newspaper delivered by bird announced that the Bloody Butchers were all given epithets if they did not already have them. "Siren of the Grand Line" Calliope, "Crustation Prince" Gibbs, and "Frost Giant" Pleco. Cas' epithet of "Bloody Butcher" was also reinstated.

Then, as the crew traveled towards Lilypad Island, they encountered the Hardshell Pirates, led by Gibbs Kanijiru, our Gibbs' father.

The Hardshell pirates rammed the Cadaver's Cleaver, Kanijiru boarded, and the two fishmen had a tense reunion. Kanijiru offered Gibbs a place on his ship so that they could sail the seas together as Crustacean King and Prince, but Gibbs refused, claiming that he had no reason to give up his real family in the Bloody Butchers.

Kanijiru and Gibbs agreed to settle their feud claw to claw in battle. In an amazing show of skill, Gibbs handily beat his much stronger and more experienced father in the fighting style that he created, causing the Hardshell pirates to quickly leave after the embarrassment of having their captain beat so badly.

Gibbs, having faced the man that left him and his mother behind and sworn to one day kill him and take his title, proved that he had the strength to protect his real family.

Arc Five: The Redline

A short time later, a terrible and dense line of smoke appeared on the horizon in the direction of the redline, pushing two marine ships with motionless, grey-skinned marines standing on the decks.

The ships caught the Cadaver’s Cleaver, and the Horseman of Death spoke to the crew through a cloud of black smoke, telling them to meet him at the redline in one week's time.

Confused why Death didn't just kill them outright and take the War fruit, the Bloody Butchers rushed to Davey Jones' Locker as the few ships they saw on the way suspiciously avoided them.

In the Locker, the crew placed the War fruit in the pedestal that originally held the Quetzalcoatl sword. Davey Jones' power activated, but then the crew had a vision of the Horseman of War, a huge armored humanoid drenched in blood. Davey Jones assured them that the War fruit was now as dormant as it could be and that it didn't seem to want to reunite with the Death fruit and end the world.

Davey Jones also told the crew that Death had already found the Famine fruit, but that it was currently impossible for him to get the last remaining fruit, Conquest. Davey Jones didn't know where Conquest was, but suspected that one of the Bloody Butchers knew where it was, even if they didn't realize it yet and that was why Death hadn't killed them yet.

With the War fruit safe and little other choice, the crew sailed to the redline to make their meeting with the Horseman of Death.

Like before, a massive cloud of black smoke appeared, pushing a ship, this time with dead pirates from each Bloody Butcher's past aboard. Also aboard was the Horseman of Death himself, in the flesh.

Death made his dramatic entrance, mocking the crew and especially calling out Calliope for not understanding how her reckless actions impact the people around her.

Then, Death sent the zombies to attack Cas. Before the rest of the Bloody Butchers could do anything to help, Cas was torn to shreds.

Cas woke on a beach with a boat ready to take him away to wherever he would go next, but out of fear of dying at sea and judgement for his many sins, he turned away and returned to the living world.

The rest of the crew saw Cas wake up, no longer with the damage to his body from the zombies, but now with a physical hole going all the way through his chest where his heart should be. The Horseman of Death laughed maniacally holding a bright white, human heart shaped devil fruit, the Horse-Horse Fruit Model: Conquest, before disappearing and leaving the crumpled corpses of the other pirates behind.

The Bloody Butchers were left in stunned silence. Cas was now undead, his heart gone but his mind and life otherwise miraculously intact. Shocked at his experience on the beach, Cas went below deck to the galley and his regular, comforting routine.

Gibbs blamed Calliope for Cas' death. Calliope was sure that Cas' death was not permanent, that there must be something that she could do to reverse it and save him.

Fifth Interlude: A Will And A Way

With Death in possession of three mythical Horse-Horse fruits and a newly undead Cas, the Bloody Butchers prepared for a final confrontation with him.

They met with Galileo again, who gave them more information on the limits of the Death fruit's power and told them that they were marked for death by the Marines. He also used the data-data fruit on the crew again, gaining all of the information that they had learned since their last encounter.

Then, the crew caught up with the Glasswerk pirates to recruit the help of Bastion and "Hidden Bullet" Petrova Nina in their plan to contain Death's power with the seastone stakes.

The crew learned that Echo died in the battle at Marine Base Borealis, throwing all of Glasswerk, but especially Bastion, into grief.

Echo's death caused Bastion to awaken his devilfruit and become much more powerful, but he also was forced to reconsider much of the way that he thought about the world. This led to a very deep conversation with and apology to Cas, who acknowledged the apology but did not forgive Bastion. However, there was room for healing.

Finally, the crew recruited the help of John Guard, Captain of the World Government Coast Guard, and his many construct boys to be a distraction for Death.

Together, everyone made a plan to create a harpoon gun for one seastone stake and let Bastion, as one of the few people who might survive touching the Horseman of Death, use the other one to give the group the most chances of successfully staking Death.

With a plan in place and a harpoon gun built by Lotus, there was nothing for us to do but wait for Death to come to them (and eat Philly cheese steaks).

Arc Six: In Death's Grasp

The Horseman of Death arrived, forming a dome of smoke around the Cadaver's Cleaver, cutting off all chance of escape. He descended from the sky on a twisted, half horse formed of smoke, arms up in celebration of his own arrival.

Cas felt a pull from his own heart, the Conquest fruit, finally within proximity of his body for the first time in a week.

Death demanded that the crew tell him the location of the War fruit, but Calliope mocked him and refused to tell him.

The Horseman of Death attacked with tendrils of dark smoke while the crew attempted to get the Famine and Conquest fruits away from Death and create an opening for Bastion or Lotus to stake him.

Pleco tried to roundhouse kick through Death, but it caused the muscles in his leg to begin withering away. Later, he threw one of John Guard's boys at the Famine fruit and told him to eat it. The boy did and was killed, but the Famine fruit was disabled.

Gibbs and Cas worked together to get the Famine fruit overboard into the ocean where Death could not follow, then Pleco, with the help of another boy, did the same with the Conquest fruit.

Then, there was an opening, Nina used her devilfruit to transport Bastion behind the Horseman of Death as he faced down Lotus' harpoon gun.

Lotus, acting on her own for the first time in her life, shot the gun, causing Death to step to the side, directly into Bastion's reach.

Bastion slammed down the seastone stake into the torso of the Horseman of Death. A flash of bright light took over the whole ship as the sea itself and the strongest devilfruit ever made did battle.

Finally, the light dissipated, and all that was left was Bastion, standing over Lamb, who had a stake directly through his stomach.

Epilogue: Being Alive Doesn't Mean You Can't Also Be Dead

The Bloody Butchers collected the Famine and Conquest fruits from the ocean and stabilized Lamb as best they could before they rushed to Davey Jones.

Davey Jones used his own power to remove the Death fruit from Lamb and seal its power, leaving the Horse-Horse Fruit Model: Death dormant.

Davey Jones explained that Lamb's soul was consumed when the Death fruit took over his body. A person can't live without a soul, but a devilfruit is a manifestation of a person’s soul, its just not your own, so they can be used as a kind of replacement to sustain a person.

To give Lamb another chance at life at the crew’s request, Davey Jones used his power to transfer the Dog-Dog Fruit Model: Hyena that the crew found at Marine Base Borealis into Lamb.

With the Death fruit dormant, the power that was sustaining Cas’ life disappeared and both he and the Conquest fruit began to fade. Despite their desperate wishes, Gibbs, Calliope, and Pleco could not help Cas.

Cas found himself on the same beach that he went to before. He considered sailing away and moving on, but the fear of his sins and the desire to stay with his friends and loved ones was too great, so he turned away from the boatman a second time and returned to life.

With the strength of his will, Cas adopted his own devilfruit, the Blood-Blood fruit, as a replacement for his soul.

With Death defeated, the original purpose of the Bloody Butchers was gone, but the many enemies they made along the way, particularly the Marines, were still after them. The crew had to face the fact that they didn't have the power to take on the Marines as they were, so the safest course of action was to split up and lie low.

Over one last meal at a tavern in Indigo Village, the same place where their journey together truly began, the Bloody Butchers exchanged goodbyes before going their separate ways.

Where to Next?

The world is intact thanks to the Bloody Butchers, but the crew is separated. Each member has grown and learned in the two years that they ended up staying apart. But now, a mysterious letter from the crew's diplomat, "Siren of the Grand Line" Calliope, has summoned them together once again. When old and new faces are reunited, will they leave a legacy worth remembering, or will they be snuffed out by the boot of the World Government?
