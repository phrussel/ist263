Welcome to the Curse of Davey Jones!

The Curse of Davey Jones is the first campaign in a collaborative storytelling and tabletop roleplaying game (ttrpg) that ran from September 2024 to February 2026. The story follows a ragtag crew of pirates as they fight to prevent the end of the world at the hands of the Horseman of Death.

This website will provide information about the campaign, a guide to the characters, and a full summary of the story of the first campaign. Thank you for reading and I hope you enjoy exploring!

The Setting

This story takes place in the world of the long-running pirate adventure manga, One Piece. Don’t worry if you’ve never read it! I haven’t either! While our story is set in the world of One Piece, it also takes inspiration from many other examples of pirate media! We sail the seas, seeking freedom and adventure while getting into battles with the marines. The important thing to know is that devilfruits are essentially superpowers that people can have. Devilfruits are created from a dead person’s willpower and they can sometimes even have wills of their own.

Game Mechanics

The game mechanics that we use for this story are a heavily simplified version of Dungeons and Dragons with homebrew mechanics to fit the setting of One Piece.

On a very basic level the game works like this; our whole group works together to roleplay and create a fun and interesting story together. If a player wants their character to do something risky (fight an enemy, search a room, endure a hard situation), then they roll a twenty-sided die (a d20) and the game master will determine if they rolled high enough for their character to succeed in what they were doing.

Player characters have six main stats: strength, dexterity, constitution, intelligence, willpower, and charisma. These stats determine the modifiers that are added to or subtracted from a player’s dice rolls. Other circumstances can also impact a player’s rolls, like if they have the element of surprise, are particularly skilled at doing something, or if they’re getting help from another player.

We use the dice to add an element of uncertainty to the story as we play. Sometimes failing at an action leads to a much more interesting story! However, we tend to prioritize the story and our fun over the game mechanics, so if someone has a really fun idea of something they want to do, usually we are willing to “break the rules” of our own game. In some ttrpg communities, this is referred to as the “Rule of Cool”.
